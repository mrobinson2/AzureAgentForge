## Contract key
- **C1 — Honesty labeling (P1):** Every simulated surface carries an on-screen demonstration disclaimer.
- **C2 — Trust receipt with deferral (P2):** Consequential AI output lists what it did AND where it deferred to a human (receipt family incl. citations/sources-on-record).
- **C3 — Refusal with a human path (P3):** At least one refusal moment, and refusals route to a named human, never a dead end.
- **C4 — Approval gate with decided states (P4):** Where owner-side actions exist: approve/adjust/decline with decided-state outcomes.
- **C5 — Simulated outcomes labeled:** Interactive outcomes (sent/booked/posted) say '(simulated)' so a demo never claims a live effect.
- **C6 — [hidden] guard:** If markup relies on the hidden attribute, CSS must guard it: author display rules silently defeat [hidden].
- **C7 — A11y basics:** Skip link + at least one ARIA state/live region (a floor, not a full audit).

- **C9 — Sealed record integrity (P7):** Pages that SEAL records must carry the append-only rule (supersede/change-order language) and customer-facing verification verdicts.
- **C10 — Movement-log transparency (P8):** Pages showing a queue position must name the cause of every movement and refuse invented dates (honest windows).
- **C11 — Charter amendment asymmetry (P9):** Pages presenting a governance charter/never-list must carry the asymmetry: tightening instant, loosening signed, versions succeeded not edited.
